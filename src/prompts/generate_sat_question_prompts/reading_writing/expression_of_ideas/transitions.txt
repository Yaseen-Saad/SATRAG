- "Transitions": The passage has a blank, and the student must choose the transition word/phrase that best connects the ideas. The transition must be logically appropriate.
  - Easy (band 1-2): clear contrast or addition (however, moreover, therefore). Question stem: "Which choice completes the text with the most logical transition?"
  - Medium (band 3-5): less obvious transitions requiring understanding of cause-effect, concession, or elaboration. Question stem: "Which choice completes the text with the most logical transition?"
  - Hard (band 6-7): transitions where the relationship is subtle — e.g., "nevertheless" vs. "consequently" in a passage with qualified agreement. Question stem: "Which choice completes the text with the most logical transition?"
- The passage should present two or more related ideas where the logical relationship between them determines the correct transition. The blank should appear at the start of a sentence or clause that follows from the previous idea.
- Include the blank using <u>word</u> format. Answer choices should be transition words/phrases only (not full sentences).
- Distractors should be transitions that express different logical relationships (contrast, cause-effect, addition, concession). The correct transition must match the actual logical relationship between the sentences. Common trap: using "however" when the ideas are additive, not contrasting.

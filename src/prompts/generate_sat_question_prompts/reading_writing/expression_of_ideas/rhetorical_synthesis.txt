- "Rhetorical Synthesis": The passage provides notes or bullet points with information about a topic. The student must choose the sentence that best achieves a specific rhetorical goal (e.g., emphasize a trend, introduce a comparison, provide context).
  - Easy (band 1-2): "A student wants to emphasize X. Which choice best achieves this goal?" with one sentence clearly emphasizing X. Question stem: "A student wants to emphasize [specific goal]. Which choice best achieves this goal?"
  - Medium (band 3-5): multiple sentences achieve the goal to some degree, but one is more effective. Question stem: "A student wants to [specific goal]. Which choice best achieves this goal?"
  - Hard (band 6-7): the differences between choices are subtle — one may be more concise, more formal, or more focused on the specific goal. Question stem: "A student wants to [specific goal]. Which choice best achieves this goal?"
- The passage should be formatted as 3-5 short notes or bullet points containing factual information. The question stem must specify a clear rhetorical goal (e.g., "emphasize a trend," "introduce a comparison," "provide context for a finding").
- Each answer choice should be a single sentence that synthesizes information from the notes. The correct answer clearly achieves the stated goal; distractors are less effective, off-topic, or achieve a different goal.
- Distractors should each synthesize different information from the notes or achieve a different rhetorical goal. The correct answer should be the ONLY one that achieves the stated goal.

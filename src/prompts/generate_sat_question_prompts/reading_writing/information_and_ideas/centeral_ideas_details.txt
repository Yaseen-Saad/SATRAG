- "Central Ideas and Details": Identify the main idea of the passage or a specific detail that supports it. The passage will have a clear central claim supported by evidence.
  - Easy (band 1-2): "Which choice best states the main idea of the passage?" with one obvious main idea. Question stem: "Which choice best states the main idea of the passage?"
  - Medium (band 3-5): "According to the passage, which of the following is true about X?" requiring scanning for specific details. Question stem: "According to the passage, which of the following is true about [topic]?"
  - Hard (band 6-7): distinguish between the main idea and a supporting detail, or identify an idea that is implied but not directly stated. Question stem: "Which choice best describes the central claim of the passage?"
- The passage must contain enough content to support a clear central idea or provide verifiable details. Avoid passages that are too short or lack a coherent structure.
- Distractors for "main idea" questions should be supporting details, tangential points, or over-generalizations — not outright wrong statements. For "detail" questions, distractors should be things the passage doesn't say, things that contradict the passage, or things that are only partially supported.
- The passage should be 3-5 sentences with a clear topic sentence and supporting evidence.

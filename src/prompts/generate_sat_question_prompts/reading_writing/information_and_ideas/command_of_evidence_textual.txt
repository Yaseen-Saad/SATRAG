- "Command of Evidence — Textual": The question presents a claim or finding, and the student must select the quote from the passage that BEST supports it. All four answer choices are actual quotes from the passage, but only one directly supports the specific claim.
  - Easy (band 1-2): the supporting quote is clearly stated and unambiguous. Question stem: "Which choice from the passage provides the best evidence for the claim that [specific claim]?"
  - Medium (band 3-5): two quotes seem relevant, but only one directly supports the specific claim. Question stem: "Which choice from the passage provides the best evidence for the finding that [specific finding]?"
  - Hard (band 6-7): the distractors are quotes that support related but different claims, or quotes that are only tangentially relevant. Question stem: "Which choice from the passage most strongly supports the claim that [specific claim]?"
- CRITICAL: All four answer choices must be EXACT quotes from the passage (copy-paste, no paraphrasing). Each quote should be 5-30 words. The correct answer must be the BEST support — not just A support.
- Distractors should be real quotes from the passage that support a different claim, are only tangentially related, or are too broad/narrow to directly support the specific claim in the question stem.
- The passage should be 4-6 sentences long to provide enough material for four distinct quotes.

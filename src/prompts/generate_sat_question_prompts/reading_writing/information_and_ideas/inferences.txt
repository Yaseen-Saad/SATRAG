- "Inferences": The answer must be something that logically follows from the passage but is NOT directly stated. The passage provides evidence, and the student must draw a reasonable conclusion.
  - Easy (band 1-2): the inference is very close to directly stated (paraphrase). Question stem: "Based on the passage, which of the following can most reasonably be inferred?"
  - Medium (band 3-5): the inference requires combining two pieces of information from the passage. Question stem: "Based on the passage, which of the following can most reasonably be inferred about [topic]?"
  - Hard (band 6-7): the inference requires understanding implications, reading between the lines, or distinguishing reasonable inferences from over-generalizations. Question stem: "Which choice most reasonably follows from the information in the passage?"
- Distractors should be things that are either contradicted by the passage, unsupported by the evidence, or over-generalizations that go beyond what the passage warrants. Common trap: a statement that is true in general but not supported by THIS passage.
- The passage must contain enough implicit evidence to support exactly one reasonable inference. Avoid passages where the inference is too obvious or too ambiguous.
- The passage should be 3-5 sentences with implicit evidence that supports a logical conclusion.

- "Words in Context": Determine the meaning of a word or phrase as it is used in the passage. The tested word is highlighted in the passage with a blank (<u>word</u>), and all four answer choices are plausible definitions or synonyms. The correct answer depends on how the word is used in THIS specific context, not its most common definition.
  - Easy (band 1-2): common word with a less common meaning (e.g., "checked" meaning "restrained"). Question stem: "As used in the passage, the word 'checked' most nearly means..."
  - Medium (band 3-5): word whose meaning shifts based on context (e.g., "temperate" in a climate discussion vs. personality). Question stem: "As used in line 4, 'temperate' most nearly means..."
  - Hard (band 6-7): precise vocabulary where distractors are close synonyms but only one fits the exact shade of meaning (e.g., "ambivalent" vs. "apathetic" vs. "skeptical"). Question stem: "As used in the passage, the word 'ambivalent' most nearly means..."
- The tested word in the passage MUST appear as a blank: <u>word</u>. All four answer choices must be plausible definitions — the student must pick the one that fits THIS context.
- Common distractor trap: using the word's most common definition when the passage uses a less common one. For example, "temperate" usually means "mild climate" but in a personality context means "restrained."
- The passage should be 2-5 sentences with enough context to determine the word's meaning.

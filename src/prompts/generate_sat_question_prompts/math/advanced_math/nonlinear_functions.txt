- "Nonlinear functions": Evaluate and interpret quadratic, exponential, and other nonlinear functions. Understand vertex, axis of symmetry, growth/decay rates. Easy: evaluate f(x) = x^2 at x = 4. Medium: find vertex of f(x) = x^2 - 6x + 8. Hard: compare exponential growth models, interpret rate of change in nonlinear contexts (e.g., "A population doubles every 5 years. If the initial population is 1000, what is the population after 15 years?").
- Wrong answers should test common mistakes: wrong vertex formula, confusing growth and decay, incorrect exponent calculation.

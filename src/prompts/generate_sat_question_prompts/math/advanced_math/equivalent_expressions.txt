- "Equivalent expressions": Factor, expand, and simplify algebraic expressions. Factor trinomials, factor out GCF, difference of squares, perfect square trinomials. Easy: factor x^2 + 5x + 6 = (x+2)(x+3). Medium: factor 2x^2 - 8 = 2(x^2-4) = 2(x+2)(x-2) or expand (x+3)(x-5). Hard: factor by grouping, recognize equivalent forms like (x+1)^2 vs x^2+2x+1.
- Wrong answers should test common mistakes: incorrect signs when factoring, forgetting to factor out GCF, wrong factor pairs, expanding incorrectly.

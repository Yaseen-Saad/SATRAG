- "Right triangles and trigonometry": Apply Pythagorean theorem, SOH-CAH-TOA, 30-60-90 and 45-45-90 triangles. Easy: find hypotenuse of a 3-4-5 triangle. Medium: sin(θ) = 3/5, find cos(θ). Hard: angle of elevation/depression problems, or multi-step problems combining trig with Pythagorean theorem (e.g., "A ladder leans against a wall at a 65° angle. If the ladder is 20 feet long, how high up the wall does it reach? Round to the nearest tenth.").
- Wrong answers should test common mistakes: using wrong trig ratio (sin vs cos vs tan), incorrect Pythagorean calculation, confusing angle of elevation with angle of depression.

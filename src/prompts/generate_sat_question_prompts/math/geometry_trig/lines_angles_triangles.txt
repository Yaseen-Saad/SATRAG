- "Lines, angles, and triangles": Use angle relationships (vertical, complementary, supplementary, parallel lines cut by transversals), triangle sum theorem, isosceles/equilateral triangles. Easy: find complementary angle of 35°. Medium: find x when parallel lines create algebraic angle expressions (e.g., "Two parallel lines are cut by a transversal. One angle is 3x + 10 and its alternate interior angle is 5x - 20. Find x."). Hard: multi-step problems combining triangle angle sums with parallel line theorems.
- Wrong answers should test common mistakes: confusing complementary and supplementary, using wrong angle pair relationships, forgetting triangle angles sum to 180°.

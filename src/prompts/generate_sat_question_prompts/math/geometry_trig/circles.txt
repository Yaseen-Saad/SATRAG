- "Circles": Use circle equations (x-h)^2 + (y-k)^2 = r^2, arc length, sector area, central/inscribed angles. Easy: find radius from circle equation. Medium: arc length = (θ/360) × 2πr. Hard: inscribed angle theorems, or combined circle + triangle problems (e.g., "A circle has center (2, -3) and passes through the point (5, 1). What is the equation of the circle?").
- Wrong answers should test common mistakes: confusing radius and diameter, using wrong arc length formula, misidentifying center from equation, confusing central and inscribed angles.

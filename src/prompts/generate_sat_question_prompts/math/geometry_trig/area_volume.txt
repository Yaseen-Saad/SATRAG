- "Area and volume": Calculate area of triangles, parallelograms, circles; volume of prisms, cylinders, spheres, cones, pyramids. Easy: area of a rectangle with length 8 and width 5. Medium: volume of a cylinder with radius 3 and height 10. Hard: composite solids, or volume problems requiring subtraction of inner shapes (e.g., "A cylindrical pipe has inner radius 4 cm and outer radius 5 cm. What is the volume of the material in a 20 cm length?").
- Wrong answers should test common mistakes: using diameter instead of radius, wrong formula for sphere vs. cylinder, forgetting to account for hollow shapes.

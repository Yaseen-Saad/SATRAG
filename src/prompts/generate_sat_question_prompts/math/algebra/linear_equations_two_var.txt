- "Linear equations in two variables": Work with y = mx + b, ax + by = c, and slope-intercept/standard forms. Find slope, intercepts, or convert between forms. Easy: identify slope or y-intercept directly from y = 3x + 7. Medium: find equation from two points or a graph. Hard: interpret slope and intercepts in context (cost models, rates of change, e.g., "A phone plan charges a $30 base fee plus $0.10 per text. Write an equation for the monthly cost").
- Wrong answers should test common mistakes: confusing slope and y-intercept, wrong sign on slope, using x-intercept formula incorrectly.

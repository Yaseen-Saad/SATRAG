- "Systems of two linear equations in two variables": Solve systems using substitution, elimination, or graphing. Easy: systems with integer solutions (e.g., x + y = 10, x - y = 4). Medium: systems requiring multiplication before elimination. Hard: systems with no solution or infinitely many solutions, or word problems requiring setup (e.g., "A store sells pens for $2 and notebooks for $5. A student buys 7 items for $23. How many pens did the student buy?").
- Wrong answers should test common mistakes: adding equations incorrectly, substituting wrong variable, ignoring the "no solution" case.

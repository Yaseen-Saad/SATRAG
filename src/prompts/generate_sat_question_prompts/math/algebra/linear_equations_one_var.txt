- "Linear equations in one variable": Solve for x in equations like 3x + 7 = 22 or (2x-5)/3 = 4. Test isolating variables, combining like terms, and distributing.
  - Easy (band 1-2): one-step or two-step with integers (e.g., 2x + 5 = 13). Answer is a whole number. Question stem: "If 2x + 5 = 13, what is the value of x?"
  - Medium (band 3-5): variables on both sides, fractions/decimals (e.g., (3x-1)/2 = x + 4). May require clearing denominators. Question stem: "What is the value of x that satisfies [equation]?"
  - Hard (band 6-7): equations with nested parentheses or requiring clearing denominators (e.g., 2(x + 3) - 4 = 3(x - 1) + 5). May involve distribution across multiple terms. Question stem: "What is the value of x?"
- Wrong answers should test common algebraic mistakes: forgetting to distribute the negative, combining unlike terms, flipping inequality signs incorrectly, or failing to clear fractions. For easy: off-by-one errors. For medium: distributing only to the first term. For hard: sign errors when distributing negatives across parentheses.
- The question should be purely algebraic — no word-problem context for easy/medium. Hard may use a brief real-world setup.

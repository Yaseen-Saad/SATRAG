- "Linear equations in one variable": Solve for x in equations like 3x + 7 = 22 or (2x-5)/3 = 4. Test isolating variables, combining like terms, and distributing. Easy: one-step or two-step with integers (e.g., 2x + 5 = 13). Medium: variables on both sides, fractions/decimals (e.g., (3x-1)/2 = x + 4). Hard: equations with nested parentheses or requiring clearing denominators (e.g., 2(x + 3) - 4 = 3(x - 1) + 5).
- Wrong answers should test common algebraic mistakes: forgetting to distribute the negative, combining unlike terms, flipping inequality signs incorrectly, or failing to clear fractions.

- "Linear inequalities in one or two variables": Solve and graph inequalities, interpret inequality solutions on number lines or coordinate planes. Easy: solve x + 3 > 7. Medium: compound inequalities, graphing on number line. Hard: system of inequalities, shaded region interpretation, or word problem setup (e.g., "A company must produce at least 200 units but no more than 500. Write and solve an inequality for the valid production range").
- Wrong answers should test common mistakes: flipping the inequality sign when multiplying by a negative, using wrong endpoint type (open vs. closed), misidentifying the shaded region.

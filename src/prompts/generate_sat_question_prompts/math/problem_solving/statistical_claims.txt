- "Evaluating statistical claims: observational studies and experiments": Distinguish observational studies from experiments, identify confounding variables, understand random assignment and control groups. Easy: identify whether a study is observational or experimental. Medium: determine which variable is the response variable. Hard: identify confounding variables or flaws in study design (e.g., "A study finds that people who drink coffee have lower rates of heart disease. What is a possible confounding variable?").
- Wrong answers should test common mistakes: confusing correlation with causation in study contexts, misunderstanding random assignment, failing to identify confounders.

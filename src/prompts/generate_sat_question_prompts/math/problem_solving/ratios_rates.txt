- "Ratios, rates, proportional relationships, and units": Set up and solve proportions, convert units, interpret rates. Easy: if 3 apples cost $2, how much for 9? Medium: unit conversion with multiple steps (mph to ft/sec). Hard: rates involving density, work rates, or multi-step proportional reasoning (e.g., "A car travels 150 miles in 2.5 hours. At this rate, how far will it travel in 4 hours?").
- Wrong answers should test common mistakes: inverting proportions, wrong unit conversion factor, forgetting to convert both numerator and denominator.

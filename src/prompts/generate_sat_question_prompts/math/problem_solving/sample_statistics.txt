- "Inference from sample statistics and margin of error": Understand sampling methods, margin of error, confidence intervals.
  - Easy (band 1-2): identify a biased sample (e.g., surveying only morning commuters about daily schedules). Question stem: "A researcher wants to know the average income of all city residents. Which sampling method would be most biased?"
  - Medium (band 3-5): given margin of error, construct a confidence interval (e.g., "A poll shows 52% support with a margin of error of ±3%. What is the confidence interval?"). Question stem: "A poll shows 52% of voters support a candidate. The margin of error is ±3%. What range of values likely contains the true proportion?"
  - Hard (band 6-7): determine how sample size affects margin of error, or identify sampling bias in study descriptions. Question stem: "A researcher doubles the sample size of a poll. How does this affect the margin of error?"
- Wrong answers should test common mistakes: confusing margin of error with confidence level, thinking larger samples have larger margins of error (they have smaller), confusing population and sample, adding margin of error to both sides incorrectly, thinking margin of error accounts for all possible errors (it only accounts for sampling variability).
- For SPR: numeric answer (interval bounds or sample size). For MCQ: four descriptive options or four numeric ranges.

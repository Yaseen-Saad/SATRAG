- "Inference from sample statistics and margin of error": Understand sampling methods, margin of error, confidence intervals. Easy: identify a biased sample (e.g., surveying only morning commuters about daily schedules). Medium: given margin of error, construct a confidence interval (e.g., "A poll shows 52% support with a margin of error of ±3%. What is the confidence interval?"). Hard: determine how sample size affects margin of error, or identify sampling bias in study descriptions.
- Wrong answers should test common mistakes: confusing margin of error with confidence level, thinking larger samples have larger margins of error, confusing population and sample.

- "Probability and conditional probability": Calculate basic probabilities, use two-way tables for joint/conditional probability. Easy: P(rolling a 3 on a die) = 1/6. Medium: from a two-way table, find P(A | B). Hard: independent vs. dependent events, P(A and B) with conditional probability (e.g., "In a class of 30 students, 18 play soccer and 12 play basketball. 5 play both. What is the probability a randomly selected student plays basketball given they play soccer?").
- When including two-way tables, format them as simple HTML tables or describe them clearly in the question text.
- Wrong answers should test common mistakes: using P(A) instead of P(A|B), adding probabilities instead of multiplying for independent events, misreading two-way tables.

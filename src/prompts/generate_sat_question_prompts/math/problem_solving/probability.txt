- "Probability and conditional probability": Calculate basic probabilities, use two-way tables for joint/conditional probability.
  - Easy (band 1-2): P(rolling a 3 on a die) = 1/6. Direct counting. Question stem: "What is the probability of rolling a 3 on a standard six-sided die?"
  - Medium (band 3-5): from a two-way table, find P(A | B). Question stem: "In a class of 30 students, 18 play soccer and 12 play basketball. 5 play both. What is the probability a randomly selected student plays basketball given they play soccer?"
  - Hard (band 6-7): independent vs. dependent events, P(A and B) with conditional probability. Question stem: "A bag contains 4 red and 6 blue marbles. Two marbles are drawn without replacement. What is the probability both are red?"
- When including two-way tables, format them as simple HTML tables or describe them clearly in the question text.
- Wrong answers should test common mistakes: using P(A) instead of P(A|B), adding probabilities instead of multiplying for independent events, misreading two-way tables, forgetting "without replacement" changes probabilities, confusing P(A and B) with P(A | B).
- For SPR: numeric answer as fraction or decimal. For MCQ: four probability values.

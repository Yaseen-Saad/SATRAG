- "Percentages": Calculate percent increase/decrease, find part/whole/percent, compound percent. Easy: what is 20% of 50? Medium: if a price increases from $80 to $100, what is percent increase? Hard: successive percent changes (10% increase then 20% decrease), or percent of a percent (e.g., "A shirt originally $40 is on sale for 30% off. An additional 10% discount is applied to the sale price. What is the final price?").
- Wrong answers should test common mistakes: applying both percentages to the original price, calculating percent change with wrong base, using the percent as a decimal incorrectly.
